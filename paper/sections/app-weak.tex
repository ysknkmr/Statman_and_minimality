\section{Necessity of the Axioms \texorpdfstring{$B$}{B} and \texorpdfstring{$C$}{C}}\label{app:weak}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main} requires that $\St(\alpha)\in L$, which is
guaranteed over $\BCI$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:sound}. We show that the axioms
$\axB$ and $\axC$ cannot be dropped here. \AP We use the \kl{logics}
\[
\intro*\CK=\lclos{\{\axC,\axK\}},\quad
\intro*\BK=\lclos{\{\axB,\axK\}},\quad
\intro*\BBpKI=\lclos{\{\axB,\axBp,\axK,\axI\}}
\]
and ticket entailment $\intro*\Tto=\lclos{\{\axB,\axBp,\axI,\axW\}}$.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:weak}
The following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] (Without $\axB$.) The formula $\axK$ is \kl{minimal} in $\CK$, but
  \[
  \St(\axK)=((b\to a)\to \ev{1})\to((a\to \ev{1})\to \ev{\rt})\to \ev{\rt}
  \]
  does not belong to $\CK$.
\item[(b)] (Without $\axC$.) The formula $\axI$ is \kl{minimal} in $\BBpKI$,
  but $\St(\axI)=((a\to a)\to \ev{\rt})\to \ev{\rt}$ does not belong to
  $\BBpKI$. Likewise, $\axK$ is \kl{minimal} in $\BK$ and in $\BBpKI$, but
  ${\St(\axK)\notin\BBpKI\supseteq\BK}$.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently, if $L$ is $\CK$ or a \kl{logic} between $\BK$ and $\BBpKI$,
then the translation $\St$ does not preserve \kl{minimality} in $L$, and
$L\lplus\alpha\ne L\lplus\St(\alpha)$ for $\alpha=\axK$; for $L=\BBpKI$ the
same holds also for $\alpha=\axI$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\emph{Minimality.} The \kl{one-step generalizations} of $\axK$ are $z\to b\to a$,
$a\to b\to z$, $a\to z$ and $z$, and those of $\axI$ are $z\to a$, $a\to z$
and $z$. None of them is classically valid, so none of them belongs to any
of the logics under consideration, all of which are contained in $\CL$.

(a) Let $M_4=(\{0,1,2,3\},\to,\{0\})$ be the matrix with
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
\to&0&1&2&3\\\hline
0&0&1&2&3\\
1&0&0&1&0\\
2&0&0&0&3\\
3&0&1&2&0
\end{array}
\]
A finite check shows that $M_4$ validates $\axC$ and $\axK$ (and hence also $\axI$,
which is derivable in $\CK$) but not $\axB$. The set $\{0\}$ is closed
under modus ponens, since $0\to y=0$ implies $y=0$, so every theorem of
$\CK$ takes the value $0$. However, under the valuation $a=b=1$,
$\ev{1}=2$ and $\ev{\rt}=3$ we have $b\to a=0$, $(b\to a)\to \ev{1}=2$, $a\to \ev{1}=1$
and $(a\to \ev{1})\to \ev{\rt}=0$, so $\St(\axK)$ takes the value
$2\to(0\to3)=2\to3=3\ne0$.

(b) Let $M_3=(\{0,1,2\},\to,\{0\})$, where $x\to y=0$ if $y\le x$ and
$x\to y=1$ otherwise. A finite check shows that $M_3$ validates $\axB$, $\axBp$,
$\axK$ and $\axI$. As before, $\{0\}$ is closed under modus ponens, so every
theorem of $\BBpKI$ takes the value $0$ in $M_3$. However, under the
valuation $a=0$ and $\ev{\rt}=2$, the formula $\St(\axI)$ takes the value
$(0\to2)\to2=1\to2=1$. The formula $\St(\axK)$ is refuted similarly. (The
matrix $M_3$ refutes $\axC$ and $\axW$.)
\end{proof}

Ticket entailment $\Tto$ contains neither $\axC$ nor $\axK$, and the
matrix $M_3$ does not apply to it, since $M_3$ refutes $\axW$. We do not
know whether $\St(\alpha)\in\Tto$ for every $\alpha\in\Tto$.
