\section{Concluding Remarks}\label{sec:conclusion}

We showed that Jeřábek's polarized extension-variable translation, with its
``$\wedge1$'' wrappers removed, preserves minimality with
respect to the substitution preorder in any logic that contains
the axiom $I$ and $\St(\alpha)$. As a consequence, $\CL$-minimal
axiomatizations of implicational logics over $\mathbf{BCI}$ can be taken of
order $4$, and \cite[Open Problem~2]{NM21} has an affirmative answer.

Open problems:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Which intermediate logics are axiomatizable by $\CL$-minimal formulas
  \cite[Open Problem~1]{NM21}? By Corollary~\ref{cor:normal-form} it
  suffices to consider formulas of order $4$.
\item Is there a minimality-preserving translation over $\mathbf{FL_e}$ with
  additive connectives, or over the non-commutative $\mathbf{FL}$
  (Section~\ref{sec:additive})?
\item Does the translation (possibly with reordered premises) preserve
  minimality over ticket entailment $\mathbf{T}_\to=\mathbf{BB'IW}$
  (Section~\ref{sec:weak})?
\item What is the least number of top-level premises of order $3$ in a
  $\CL$-minimal order-$4$ axiom of a proper intermediate logic? It is at
  least $2$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:one-premise}), and $\St(G')$ has $11$.
\end{enumerate}
