\section{Formalization in Lean}\label{sec:lean}

\plot{Half a page.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, Lemma~\ref{lem:sound}, the \kl{order} bound of
Corollary~\ref{cor:normal-form},
Corollary~\ref{cor:many-logics} and the explicit formula of
Corollary~\ref{cor:LC} have been formalized in the
Lean~4 proof assistant~\cite{Lean}. The development consists of a single
file, \texttt{lean/StatmanMinimality.lean}, of about 1300 lines. It uses
only the Lean core library, without Mathlib, and contains neither
\texttt{sorry} nor \texttt{native\_decide}; the command
\texttt{\#print axioms} reports only the standard axioms \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}. (The identifiers in the
formalization use the name \texttt{statman} for the translation $\St$.)

Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is formalized in exactly the generality stated. A
logic is represented by a predicate \texttt{Th} on formulas satisfying
\texttt{PreLogic Th}, which expresses closure under substitution and modus
ponens together with all instances $\gamma\to\gamma$ of $\axI$. The main
theorem reads
\begin{quote}
\ttfamily statman\_minimalIn : PreLogic Th $\to$\\
\hspace*{2em}($\forall$ q, Occ q A $\to$ q < N) $\to$ MinimalIn Th A $\to$\\
\hspace*{2em}Th (statman A N) $\to$ MinimalIn Th (statman A N),
\end{quote}
where the hypothesis on \texttt{N} ensures that the extension variables are
fresh. The classical instance \texttt{statman\_minimal} is derived from it
by means of \texttt{logic\_valid} and \texttt{statman\_valid}.

Lemma~\ref{lem:sound} and the decoding argument in the proof of
Corollary~\ref{cor:many-logics} are formalized for
every logic containing $\BCI$, represented by
\texttt{BCIExt Th} (closure under substitution and modus ponens, and all
instances of $\axB$, $\axC$ and $\axI$), as
\begin{quote}
\ttfamily BCIExt.statman\_imp : Th (A $\to$ statman A N),\\
BCIExt.statman\_iff : ($\forall$ q, Occ q A $\to$ q < N) $\to$\\
\hspace*{2em}(Th A $\leftrightarrow$ Th (statman A N)),
\end{quote}
from which the equation $L\lplus\alpha=L\lplus\St(\alpha)$ of
Corollary~\ref{cor:many-logics} follows.
\begin{sloppypar}
\noindent The theorem \texttt{BCIExt.statman\_minimal} is
Corollary~\ref{cor:many-logics}, without further hypotheses. We have also
formalized the \kl{order} bound $\Ord(\St(\alpha))\le4$ as
\texttt{statman\_ord}, and the
explicit formula of Corollary~\ref{cor:LC}: \texttt{statman\_Gprime}
states that the displayed formula $\St(G')$ equals
\texttt{statman Gprime 8}, and \texttt{SGprime\_ord} states that it has
\kl{order}~$4$. The results of Section~\ref{sec:fl} have not been
formalized.
\end{sloppypar}

The formal proof follows the paper closely. The lemmas \texttt{reduction}
and \texttt{strict} correspond to Proposition~\ref{cor:criterion}, and
\texttt{decode} and \texttt{decode\_node} correspond to
Lemma~\ref{lem:key}; the latter are organized node by node rather than via
the reduction to basic generalizations of Lemma~\ref{lem:normal}.
